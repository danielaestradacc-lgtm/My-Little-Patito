# Merlin the Duck

A cute desktop pet made with Godot. A pixel art duck that lives on top of your windows, walks around your screen, and takes little naps while you work.

## How it works

Merlin walks all over your screen randomly, turning around at the edges of your display or whenever he feels like changing direction. Every now and then, he gets a little tired and stops to rest. 

Sometimes he falls fast asleep—you will know he is sleeping when you see little stars around him! When this happens, simply pick him up with your mouse cursor to wake him up and get him moving again. You can drag him around your desktop anytime, but keep in mind that he loves walking and will get right back to exploring as soon as you let go.

## Controls

* **Left click and drag:** Pick up Merlin to move him around or wake him up when he is sleeping.
